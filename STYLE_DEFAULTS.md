# Reusable creator preferences

Add a preference here only when the creator says it applies to future videos or it recurs across projects. Project-specific notes belong in `projects/<name>/STYLE.md`.

**For open-source users:** The rows below reflect the preferences of the original creator of this kit (Kale Farm vlog project). Clear or replace them when setting up this workspace for a different creator. Do not treat these as universal defaults.

| Preference | Why / evidence | Added from project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep portrait shots upright in a 16:9 vlog frame, using soft side fill when needed. | The creator found direct 9:16 use messy in the Kale Farm vlog. | Kale Farm |
| Build a visible journey before arriving at the venue when route footage exists. | The creator asked for road, greenery, and flowers to tell how they got there. | Kale Farm |
| Use Poppins for video headings and Open Sans for supporting text unless a future brief changes the pairing. | The creator rejected the Kale Farm graphic typography and explicitly requested these two fonts for the video and editing skills. | Kale Farm |
| Use story-motivated motion and restrained transitions; review them at playback speed. | The creator found the Kale Farm accent sweeps and simple graphics poor. | Kale Farm |
